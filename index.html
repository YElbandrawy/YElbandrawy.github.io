Get In Canada

A case management system for immigration law firms

This is the biggest thing I've built. Firms use it to run their clients' applications from start to finish: documents, forms, payments and deadlines. Each firm's data stays private to that firm. I designed it and wrote most of it, from the backend and the frontend to the cloud setup it runs on.

the hard one →↓

Filling government forms without breaking them

Canada's official immigration forms are PDFs with digital signatures and barcodes built in, and the usual ways of filling them in break those. That's a known headache in the industry. After a lot of digging I found a way to fill them automatically while keeping the official forms valid, and built it into a service the whole platform uses. A crawler keeps the library of forms up to date, so new ones don't have to be set up by hand.

AI · documents

Reading documents with AI

Clients upload passports, visas and other documents. AI pulls out the details and flags anything that doesn't add up for a person to review. I spent a lot of time making it efficient, and it now runs at 4–7× lower AI cost than where it started.

Chrome extension

Autofill for government portals

Some applications have to be submitted through government websites, field by field. The extension fills those long forms in from the client's file, and it keeps working when the websites change.
